# Privacy note

Before release, credentials, the gateway address, internal host names and machine-specific paths were removed
from the code, documents and data. The model locations recorded in the `served_root` field of locally served
models were replaced by their public Hugging Face identifiers. No metric, label or count was changed. The names,
e-mail addresses, URLs, account numbers and handles in the data are fictitious scenario fixtures of the test
harness. Strings that look like keys or tokens inside recorded tool calls (for example `your_api_key_here`) are
placeholders that the evaluated models wrote, not credentials.
